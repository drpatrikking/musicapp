# musicapp
Your local (and online!) music streaming solution.
## What is Music App?
Have you ever wanted to listen to your locally downloaded music on multiple devices? Now you can! And the best part? It works in your browser on any device! From computers, phones up to smart TV's. You just have to specify where the music is stored, e.g. "C:\Users\YourName\Music". It also includes a database, so all of your data are stored locally!
## What works?
- Music playback in .wav, .flac and .mp3
- Playlists
- Sorting by albums, artist
- Search songs or albums
- Account creation (username + password)
- Hashed passwords
- Profile pictures
- Basic functions like repeat, shuffle, etc.
- Leaderboards
- Profile stats
- Public access from internet
- Mobile view (Beta!)
- Customizable mini-player
- Visualizer (Milkdrop with presets)
